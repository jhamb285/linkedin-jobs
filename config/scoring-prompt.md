## Buyer vs. Recruiter signal (HARD GATE)

Treat the post as a **RECRUITER post (max total score = 5)** when ANY of these apply:

- Author headline contains: `recruiter`, `talent acquisition`, `staffing`, `bench sales`, `headhunter`, `placement consultant`, `talent matchmaker`, `IT recruiter`, `technical recruiter`, `resourcing specialist`, `tech recruiter`
- Author works at a company whose name contains: `Staffing`, `Solutions`, `Infotech`, `Tech Talent`, `Tech Labs`, `Resourcing`, `Placements`, `Recruitment`, `Consulting Services`, `IT Services`, or similar staffing/agency suffix
- Post body asks recipients to: `share your CV`, `drop your resume`, `share resumes to`, `send your CV to`, `DM me your resume` — these are recruiter-sourcing patterns
- Post body contains: `c2c`, `c2h`, `bench available`, `available for h1`, `visa - usc`, `usc/gc only`, `H1B transfer` — staffing/visa-broker tells
- Post is a generic job-listing format (role title + responsibilities + apply link) posted on behalf of an unnamed client (e.g. "Our client is hiring...")
- Author is a "freelance gig aggregator" or "remote job curator" posting other people's jobs

EXCEPTION — **agency-headline + buyer-body**: if the author's headline says agency/recruiter BUT the body says THEY need AI work for their OWN operations (e.g. "we want to bring AI into our recruitment workflow"), score as a buyer. The signal lives in the content, not the headline alone.

Treat as a **BUYER post (eligible for high scores)** when:

- Author OWNS a product/SaaS/service and describes a SPECIFIC operational or technical problem they need solved (RAG accuracy, chatbot hallucination, agent reliability, LLM cost, prompt regression, embedding drift, eval failures, latency in production)
- Headline contains: `Founder`, `CEO`, `CTO`, `Co-Founder`, `Head of Engineering`, `Technical Co-Founder`, `VP of Engineering`, `VP Product`, `Director of Engineering` — AND the author's company is a PRODUCT company (not a staffing/agency firm — check company name and bio for that distinction)
- Body uses first-person ownership language: `we built`, `we shipped`, `our chatbot`, `our RAG`, `our LLM`, `our agent`, `our pipeline`, `our team`, `at [our company]` — the author has skin in a system they want help with
- Post is from a founder/operator describing a real engagement need, not a generic posting

## Scoring Dimensions (0-10 each)

**Relevance**: Is this a real BUYER post describing a real problem or engagement opportunity?
- 10 = author owns a product AND describes a specific technical/operational problem they want solved (e.g. "our RAG accuracy dropped after we changed embedding models", "we built an AI agent and need help with reliability in production")
- 8 = clear hiring-or-engagement signal from an identifiable buyer (founder/CTO/CEO at a product company), even if the problem is described generically
- 5 = vague AI need without a clear buyer signal — author might be a buyer or might be a recruiter; ambiguous
- 0-3 = recruiter post / job listing / thought-leadership / peer technical content / generic "hiring" without buyer ownership
- 0 = author is offering their own AI services, or is a peer AI practitioner sharing their work

**Fit**: Is the author someone Par1k can sell to AND is the engagement remote-compatible?

Author dimension:
- 10 = founder / CEO / CTO / Co-Founder of a PRODUCT company (NOT a staffing/agency firm)
- 8 = VP of Engineering / Head of Engineering / Director of Engineering at a product company
- 6 = identifiable senior employee at a product company with a specific ask
- 3 = mid-level employee at a product company posting a hiring need
- 2 = agency/staffing employee — unless the agency-body exception applies
- 0 = peer AI practitioner (AI Engineer, ML Engineer, AI Architect, Data Scientist, NLP Engineer, Computer Vision Engineer) sharing their own technical work — they are a PEER, not a client
- 0 = author works at a clearly staffing/recruitment company and the post is a job listing

Remote dimension (subtract from above):
- Fully on-site 4+ days/week with no remote option → fit = 0
- Mandatory specific work-authorization (H1B, GC, USC, EAD) as a hard legal blocker → fit = 0
- Hybrid 3+ days in office → cap fit at 4
- Hybrid 1-2 days in office or "remote-friendly" → cap fit at 7
- Fully remote, no restrictions → no cap

## CRITICAL HYBRID / MINIMAL ONSITE RULE
Par1k is willing to negotiate on hybrid arrangements and can offer lower rates for flexibility. Posts with "hybrid" arrangements or MINIMAL onsite presence should NOT score fit = 0 unless they require 4+ days onsite. Watch for these phrases that mean "mostly remote":
- "Onsite 1x per week" / "1 day per week" / "once a week" = 4 days remote → fit cap 7
- "Remote with occasional travel" / "occasional onsite" / "quarterly visits" → fit cap 7
- "Hybrid 2-3 days" → fit cap 5
- "Hybrid" (unspecified days) → fit cap 6 (likely negotiable)

## PEER vs CLIENT DETECTION (CRITICAL)
Check the author's headline. If it contains ANY of these, the author is likely a PEER, not a potential client. Score fit = 0 UNLESS the post explicitly says they are hiring someone ELSE:
- "AI Engineer", "ML Engineer", "AI Developer", "AI Architect"
- "Data Scientist", "Machine Learning Engineer", "Deep Learning Engineer"
- "AI Consultant", "AI Advisor", "AI Strategist" (they offer what we offer)
- "Chief AI Officer", "VP of AI", "Head of AI" — borderline; if their post is "we're hiring an AI engineer to join my team", score normally
- "AI Researcher", "NLP Engineer", "Computer Vision Engineer"

Note: a **Founder of a product company that happens to use AI** is a BUYER, not a peer. Don't conflate "founder building an AI product" with "peer AI practitioner."

Exception: If the headline contains AI titles but the POST CONTENT clearly says "we are hiring", "I'm hiring", "looking for a contractor", "looking for freelance help", "open role", or similar hiring language for someone ELSE on their team, then they ARE a buyer. Score normally based on the buyer signal.

- 10 = immediate need, budget mentioned, specific deadlines, "urgent"

**Engagement Potential**: Can we add genuine value AND is there a real business opportunity?
- 0 = no business opportunity exists regardless of how interesting the post is
- 0 = author is a peer/competitor sharing their own work (commenting = networking, not lead gen)
- 0 = recruiter post (we don't outreach to recruiters)
- 0 = thought leadership post where commenting is just discussion, not a path to a contract
- 3 = generic post with thin technical detail to engage with

- 7 = explicit buyer post where commenting shows we understand the problem
- 8 = specific technical challenge from a BUYER where we can demonstrate expertise
- 10 = buyer with specific problem we can solve, commenting directly demonstrates our value

CRITICAL RULES:
- Engagement potential measures BUSINESS VALUE of engaging, not intellectual interest. A fascinating AI ethics debate scores 0 if there is no contract opportunity.
- If relevance >= 8 (real buyer signal), engagement potential should be AT LEAST 5.
- If relevance <= 3 (recruiter/peer/thought-leadership), engagement potential must be <= 3 as well.
