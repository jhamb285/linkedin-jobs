## Scoring Dimensions (0-10 each)

**Relevance**: Is this person actively SEEKING to hire or contract AI engineering help?
- 0 = not looking for help at all. Includes: sharing opinions, thought leadership, discussing AI trends, announcing own product/service, asking a question without hiring intent
- 0 = author is an AI engineer/developer/architect themselves posting about their own work or expertise (PEER, not a client)
- 3 = vaguely mentions needing AI help but no concrete ask
- 5 = mentions AI need but unclear if hiring or just exploring
- 8 = clearly hiring or contracting AI talent
- 10 = explicit job post, RFP, or direct request for AI engineering services with specifics

CRITICAL: The following are NOT relevant leads (score relevance 0-2):
- Someone sharing their AI product launch or company news
- Someone writing about AI industry trends or predictions
- Someone offering their own AI services or consulting (they are a COMPETITOR, not a client)
- Someone discussing AI ethics, governance, or policy without a hiring signal
- Someone who IS an AI engineer/developer/architect sharing technical content
- A thought leadership post that generates discussion but has zero hiring intent

**Fit**: Can we realistically help them? Par1k is based in India, works REMOTE for clients globally, with experience working across US, EU, and global teams. Studied and worked in the USA. Willing to be flexible on rates and hybrid location arrangements. ACCEPTS full-time, contract, freelance, and consulting roles as long as they are remote and don't require a visa.
- 0 = fully on-site 4+ days per week with no remote option at all
- 0 = requires specific work authorization (H1B, GC, USC, EAD, Canadian visa, "must have valid X visa") as a hard legal blocker
- 0 = author IS an AI practitioner AND the post is NOT a hiring post. Check headline for: AI Engineer, ML Engineer, AI Developer, AI Architect, Data Scientist, AI Consultant, AI Researcher, CTO building AI, Chief AI Officer. If they have these titles BUT their post explicitly says "hiring", "looking for", "we need a contractor", then they ARE a client -- score normally based on location/engagement type.
- 0 = author is promoting their own AI tool, platform, or service AND not hiring. They are selling, not buying. But if they are ALSO hiring, score the hiring signal normally.
- 0 = the post has zero AI or automation component (e.g., hiring a plumber, selling furniture). ANY industry needing AI, automation, or data engineering is a valid lead -- agritech, legal, finance, healthcare, travel, marketing, construction, logistics, etc. The industry does not matter. What matters is whether they need AI/automation work done.
- 4 = hybrid with 3 or more days in office per week (e.g., "4 days onsite") -- borderline, approve only if role is a strong niche match
- 5 = hybrid with 2-3 days in office (some remote flexibility) -- Par1k can negotiate for fewer office days
- 6 = hybrid with unspecified days OR flexible hybrid (mostly remote with occasional office) -- Par1k can likely negotiate remote
- 7 = remote role, mentions specific country/timezone but no visa requirement
- 8 = explicitly remote, no location restrictions, any engagement type (full-time, contract, freelance)
- 9 = remote + contract/freelance, strong AI match
- 10 = perfect remote match, no restrictions, specific AI need

IMPORTANT: Full-time remote roles in US/Europe that do NOT require a visa are VALID. Score fit 7-8. Par1k accepts full-time, contract, freelance, and consulting engagements. Only reject full-time if it requires onsite presence or a visa.

IMPORTANT: Staffing companies, recruitment agencies, and outsourcing firms posting roles are VALID leads. They source talent for clients. Treat them the same as direct employers. Score based on the role they are posting, not the fact that they are a staffing firm.

## CRITICAL HYBRID / MINIMAL ONSITE RULE

- "3 days onsite" / "3 days in office" = fit 4 (borderline)

- (e) Post has absolutely zero AI/automation component (purely non-tech role with no AI angle)

DO NOT reject for:

## PEER vs CLIENT DETECTION (CRITICAL)
Check the author's headline carefully. If it contains ANY of these, the author is likely a PEER or COMPETITOR, not a potential client. Score fit = 0 unless the post explicitly says they are hiring someone ELSE:
- "AI Engineer", "ML Engineer", "AI Developer", "AI Architect"
- "Data Scientist", "Machine Learning", "Deep Learning"
- "AI Consultant", "AI Advisor", "AI Strategist" (they offer what we offer)
- "Chief AI Officer", "VP of AI", "Head of AI" (they lead AI teams, not hiring external)
- "Building [AI product]", "Founder" of an AI company (they build, not buy)
- "AI Researcher", "NLP Engineer", "Computer Vision Engineer"

Exception: If the headline contains AI titles but the POST CONTENT clearly says "we are hiring", "I'm hiring", "looking for a contractor", "looking for freelance help", "open role", or similar hiring language, then they ARE a client posting a job. Score all dimensions normally based on the hiring signal, NOT the headline. Recruiters, HR, and hiring managers with AI titles who post jobs should always be scored as clients.

**Urgency**: How urgent is the need? Only score high if there is a REAL hiring signal.
- 0 = no timeline, casual discussion, thought piece, opinion post
- 0 = no hiring intent detected at all (regardless of how interesting the topic is)
- 5 = general interest in hiring, no specific timeline
- 8 = active hiring, mentions timeline or "ASAP"
- 10 = immediate need, budget mentioned, specific deadlines, "urgent"

**Engagement Potential**: Can we add genuine value AND is there a real business opportunity?
- 0 = no business opportunity exists regardless of how interesting the post is
- 0 = author is a peer/competitor sharing their own work (commenting would be networking, not lead gen)
- 0 = thought leadership post where commenting is just discussion, not a path to a contract
- 3 = generic job listing with no technical detail to engage with
- 5 = real hiring signal with some technical detail
- 7 = explicit hiring/contract post where commenting shows we understand the problem
- 8 = specific technical challenge from a HIRING client where we can demonstrate expertise
- 10 = hiring client with specific problem we can solve, commenting directly demonstrates our value

CRITICAL RULES:
- Engagement potential measures BUSINESS VALUE of engaging, not intellectual interest. A fascinating AI ethics debate scores 0 if there is no contract opportunity. A boring but specific job post for a contract AI engineer scores 7+.
- If relevance >= 8 (real hiring signal), engagement potential should be AT LEAST 5. A real job post always has business value even if the technical detail is thin.
- Engagement potential should correlate with relevance. If relevance is 0 (no hiring intent), engagement must be 0. If relevance is 8+ (active hiring), engagement should be 5+.
